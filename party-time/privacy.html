Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Party Time is an Apple Watch app published by Gamla Ridge LLC ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. Party Time does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or network connections.

Microphone

If you turn on Sync to Sound, Party Time asks for permission to use your watch's microphone. Sound is processed live, on your watch only, to measure loudness and find the beat. Audio is never recorded, saved to a file or sent anywhere, and listening stops whenever the app is not active. You can turn Sync to Sound off at any time, or revoke microphone access in the watch's Settings app.

Settings stored on your watch

Your chosen scene, microphone sensitivity, strobe intensity and Sync to Sound preference are saved on your watch so they persist between launches. They never leave your device.

Children

Party Time does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
